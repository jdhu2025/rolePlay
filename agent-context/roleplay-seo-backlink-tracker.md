## Status Legend

- `prospect`: candidate found, not contacted/submitted yet.
- `submitted`: form submission completed.
- `outreach_sent`: direct email/DM sent.
- `followed_up`: at least one follow-up sent.
- `live`: link or mention is live.
- `rejected`: declined, unsuitable, or removed.
- `blocked`: needs account, payment decision, CAPTCHA, login, or user approval.
