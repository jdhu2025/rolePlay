## Product Boundaries

Keepsay is a fictional character creation and interactive storytelling product. Users can create original fictional characters, continue story scenes, save story memory, preview voices, and generate policy-safe character images.

Keepsay is not a dating service, adult chatbot, NSFW chatbot, escort service, sexual service, adult entertainment service, face-swap product, or deepfake tool.

## Content Boundaries

Keepsay prohibits NSFW, pornographic, explicit, sexually suggestive, exploitative, illegal, harassing, hateful, or harmful content. Users may not create or market characters as AI girlfriends, AI boyfriends, adult companions, dating partners, or sexual chatbots.

Characters, conversations, voices, and generated images must remain fictional, non-explicit, respectful, and policy-safe.

## Image Generation Rules

Generated character images must be fictional, fully clothed, non-explicit, and clearly adult when depicting adults. Users may not request nudity, sexualized poses, bedroom-focused sexual scenarios, minor-coded characters, face swaps, deepfakes, impersonation, or non-consensual likeness use.

Image and video prompts may be screened before generation. If a prompt is denied or flagged by moderation, generation is blocked. If required moderation is unavailable, generation may be temporarily blocked.

## Moderation And Enforcement

Keepsay uses safety controls to screen prompts, limit risky public surfaces, and enforce the Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy. We may block prompts, remove content, limit features, suspend accounts, terminate access, or report unlawful activity when policy violations occur.
